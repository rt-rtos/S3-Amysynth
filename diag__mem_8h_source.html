2/* Where does a given allocation actually live?
3 *
4 * On the S3 the address is the region, so classifying a pointer is a range
5 * compare - which makes this the only reliable answer to questions an allocator
6 * attribute cannot settle. A MALLOC_CAP_SPIRAM request silently falls back to
7 * internal RAM under pressure; the gamma9001 drum blob is flash-mapped or
8 * staged in PSRAM depending on whether MMU pages were available at boot; and
9 * under LTO an IRAM attribute in source proves nothing about where the linker
10 * put the code.
11 *
12 * Placement does not change after init, so the intended use is to track the
13 * notable allocations as they are made and print the map once, not to poll.
14 *
15 * Cost when unused: the tracking table (DIAG_MEM_MAX_ENTRIES * 12 bytes of
16 * DRAM) and nothing else. Deliberately not Kconfig-gated - a placement check
17 * you have to rebuild to enable is one you will not run.
18 */
