2/* Periodic diagnostic reporting.
3 *
4 * The app's idle loop calls diag_report_all() on an interval and each enabled
5 * diagnostic prints its window. Reporters are registered rather than called
6 * directly so that adding or gating one never touches the loop: the pile of
7 * per-diagnostic #if blocks that used to sit in app_main is now a single call.
8 *
9 * Reporters run in registration order, sequentially, on the calling task. They
10 * must be Core-0 / low-priority safe: reporting walks task tables, formats
11 * strings and may briefly allocate, so nothing here may be called from the
12 * render path, a tick hook or an ISR.
13 *
14 * diag_init() registers the reporters that live inside this component (render
15 * timing, RTOS stats, AMY profile dumps), each self-gating on its own Kconfig
16 * symbol. Other components register their own from their init path.
17 */
