12/* ── Track Options screen renderer ────────────────────────────────────────
13 * Per-track / per-layer secondary parameters. Layer and track are
14 * selectable directly on this screen (rows 0-1 rendered in the title bar):
15 * TRACK OPTS [L2] T1 ← inverted when cursor==TO_ROW_LAYER
16 * TRACK OPTS L2 [T1] ← inverted when cursor==TO_ROW_TRACK
17 * ─────────────────────
18 * Repeat Rate : 2
19 * Mute : OFF
20 * Solo : OFF
21 * Chord Mode : ON (melodic layers only)
22 * Root : A
23 * Type : Min7
24 * Cursor rows 2..7 are the content rows; rows 5..7 (chord/root/type) hidden
25 * for drum layers. Only 4 content rows are visible at once — the list
26 * scrolls (same windowing as the drone screen) to keep the cursor in view
27 * on melodic layers, which have 6 content rows. */
