13/* ── Stutter house drone ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
14 * A standalone drone synth, independent of the sequencer layers and the arp:
15 * its own AMY synth slots (above the 0..63 map), state and screen.
16 *
17 * Sound design (WAVE mode):
18 * osc0 = NOTE-following carrier, one voice per chord note, amplitude gated
19 * via mod_source by a continuously-running square LFO. AMY's dB/exp
20 * amp model: amp = const_sent * 10^(3*m*LFO), LFO in {-1,+1}, giving
21 * peak_lin on-beat and peak_lin * 10^(-duck_db/20) off-beat. Knob
22 * mapping: drone_set_amp_peak / drone_set_amp_duck.
23 * osc1 = PULSE LFO at a tempo-locked division (the "stutter rate").
24 * LPF24 cutoff swept slowly, tempo-locked in bars.
25 * A single "sub" voice tracks the chord ROOT a fixed interval below.
26 *
27 * PATCH mode loads an AMY patch instead of the raw 2-osc voice, so the patch
28 * owns its oscillators and amplitude: the stutter LFO and const/mod amp
29 * controls are WAVE-only, while chord voicing, filter sweep and resonance
30 * still apply.
31 *
32 * LFO frequency and sweep period both derive from the global BPM. */
33
