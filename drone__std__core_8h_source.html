13/* ── Normal (free-running) drone ──────────────────────────────────────────
14 * The stutter drone's sibling: the same chord voicing (one voice per chord
15 * note on the main synth) and mono sub an interval below the root, but with
16 * a plain sustained output — no stutter gate, no peak/duck amp coupling, no
17 * pattern/blip/swing service and no tempo-locked filter sweep.
18 *
19 * In their place it exposes the standard per-voice toolset the melodic
20 * tracks and the arp already have:
21 * - a free filter (seq_filter_t: type/cutoff/resonance/enable) edited in
22 * the full filter graph editor and pushed via sequencer_core_push_filter;
23 * - a free AMY-native LFO (seq_lfo_t) applied with voice_apply_native_lfo
24 * (WAVE mode only — PATCH-mode instruments own their osc topology);
25 * - the shared ADSR/EG1 graph editor storage.
26 *
27 * It owns AMY synth slots 66/67 (above the stutter drone's 64/65) so both
28 * drones can sound simultaneously; amy_cfg.max_synths must be >= 68.
29 * All AMY interaction goes through amy_helpers deltas (never amy_queue_lock),
30 * matching drone_core's concurrency model. */
