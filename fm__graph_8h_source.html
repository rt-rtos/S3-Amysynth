11 * Pure functions (no AMY, no RTOS): host-testable. An operator program is
12 * AMY's per-slot routing byte array (algorithms.c FmOperatorFlags); a graph
13 * is who-modulates-whom over the 6 operators.
14 *
15 * Index convention: operator i is AMY algo_source slot i in table mode and
16 * osc i+1 of the voice always. The UI labels it OP(6-i), matching DX7 charts
17 * (table rows are authored DX7 op6 first). */
