9/* Transport-agnostic MIDI byte-stream parser + router.
10 *
11 * Transports (ble_midi today, usb_midi later) strip their framing and feed
12 * plain MIDI bytes here; complete note messages go to the application's sink.
13 * Everything except note-on/note-off is parsed (keeping running status
14 * coherent) and dropped.
15 *
16 * Threading: SINGLE PRODUCER, currently the NimBLE host task. A second
17 * transport must share that task or add its own serialization before calling
18 * midi_core_feed(). The sink runs in the producer's context, so it must be
19 * cheap and cross-task safe (the live_play sink goes through the bounded
