11/*
12 * render_clock — the master clock for the audio render loop.
13 *
14 * Emits exactly one "render now" signal per audio block period (256 samples
15 * @ 48 kHz = 5333.333 µs), independent of the FreeRTOS tick rate. Two backends
16 * implement this header, selected by CONFIG_RENDER_CLOCK_I2S_ENABLE (default
17 * OFF):
18 *
19 * - render_clock.c (default): GPTimer alarm ISR on the render task's core,
20 * waking it via a FreeRTOS task notification.
21 *
22 * - render_clock_i2s.c (opt-in): I2S TX channel with no GPIO assigned;
23 * render_clock_wait() blocks on i2s_channel_write() and DMA backpressure
24 * paces render. Its DMA queue depth (CONFIG_RENDER_CLOCK_I2S_DMA_DESC_NUM)
25 * gives look-ahead slack the GPTimer path lacks. The render task body is
26 * unchanged either way; only this seam differs.
27 *
28 * STRICT 1:1: the caller renders exactly ONE block per render_clock_wait()
29 * return, never the backlog, so AMY's total_blocks stays locked to realtime and
30 * the sequencer tempo (from amy_sysclock()) cannot drift. Both backends.
31 */
