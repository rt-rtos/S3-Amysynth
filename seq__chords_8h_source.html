3/* ── Chord presets ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4 * A global table of user-authored chords (absolute MIDI pitches) that melodic
5 * tracks can play instead of a single note. Assignment rides the per-track
6 * source-note path: SEQ_CHORD_BASE..+SLOTS-1 in track_base_note / step_note[]
7 * are sentinels meaning "play chord slot N", passing through
8 * sequencer_resolve_track_note() untouched. Expansion to real pitches happens
9 * at emit time (the decorated one-shot path in seq_core_trig.c), where the
10 * progression's transpose offset is applied per fire - intervals preserved,
11 * chord tones never individually re-quantized.
12 *
13 * The sentinel range starts at 200 so 97..199 stays free for future sentinel
14 * families; step_note is uint8_t and the playable range tops out at
15 * SEQ_MEL_NOTE_MAX (96). */

24#define SEQ_CHORD_SLOTS 8
25/* Storage width: wide enough for the 9th chords. Authoring is capped below
26 * that at SEQ_CHORD_MAX_SELECT, because a chord-carrying row widens its voice
27 * count to the tone count and seq_clamp_patch_voices caps voices at
28 * SEQ_TRACK_OSC_BUDGET / oscs_per_voice, which is 32/8 = 4 for the DX7 family:
29 * a 9th on a DX7 patch would clamp to four voices and lose its top tone.
30 * Storage stays 5-wide so admitting 5-tone voicings again (e.g. by spreading
31 * tones across a track pair) is a picker-ceiling change, not a format change. */
32#define SEQ_CHORD_MAX_NOTES 5
33/* Widest chord the picker offers. Raising this past 4 re-admits the 9ths and
34 * re-opens the top-tone clamp described above. */
35#define SEQ_CHORD_MAX_SELECT 4
