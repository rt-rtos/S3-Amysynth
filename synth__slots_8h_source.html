3/* ── AMY synth slot map - single source of truth ─────────────────────────
4 * Static consumers pack the bottom of the slot space; the melodic sequencer
5 * is an open-ended arena on top. Growing melodic capacity is one edit:
6 * raise SYNTH_SLOT_COUNT (the real ceiling is AMY's osc pool, max_oscs).
7 *
8 * Contract:
9 * - Slot 0 is reserved as a sentinel: sequencer row lookups use synth id 0
10 * for "no such row" (seq_core_synth.c). Never assign it to a consumer.
11 * - Static slots (1..SEQ_MEL_SYNTH_BASE-1) are compile-time fixed, never
12 * overlap, and are identical across targets.
13 * - Melodic allocation runs [SEQ_MEL_SYNTH_BASE, SEQ_MAX_SYNTH]; the
14 * static-vs-melodic predicate is a single `slot >= SEQ_MEL_SYNTH_BASE`.
15 * - amy_cfg.max_synths (main.c) must be SYNTH_SLOT_COUNT - it is, by
16 * construction, the only other place this map surfaces. */
17
